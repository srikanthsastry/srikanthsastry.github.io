**When iterating on a spec, convergence against a model's confabulation pattern is indistinguishable from convergence against intent.**

The [directive-gap](/garden/directive-gap/) goes invisible when the model's [confabulations](/garden/confabulation-is-plausible/) fill gaps in ways that match intent. Convergence is real but fragile: against this model's pattern, not a model-independent spec. When model changes, the gap reappears. Wasowski's test surviving four generations while a 1,500-word spec needed reinterpretation shows the split: tests are formal, specs are model-dependent.. This is complementary to [co-artifact-model](/garden/co-artifact-model/) and [two-layer-governance-model](/garden/two-layer-governance-model/), diagnosed via [reverse-pass-observer](/garden/reverse-pass-observer/) and rooted in [suggestible-actor-properties](/garden/suggestible-actor-properties/).
