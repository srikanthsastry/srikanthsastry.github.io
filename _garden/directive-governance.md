**Information flows up (compressed and lossy), decisions are made centrally based on that compressed information, directives flow down for execution, and accountability is for compliance with directives rather than for outcomes.** The colloquial label is "command and control," but that military term is neutral and includes decentralized philosophies like mission command. "Directive governance" is more precise for the specific pattern the tech industry practices.

Structurally, directive governance is a hybrid with a mechanistic core (Burns & Stalker, 1961) and organic ornamentation. The hierarchy, formal procedures, and top-down decision-making are mechanistic. The hackathons, "innovation labs," and autonomous-team branding are decorative. The combination preserves centralized decision authority while performing the rituals of decentralization.
