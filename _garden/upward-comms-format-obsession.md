**Format obsession in upward communication displaces substance with compliance.** When leadership mandates specific templates, slide formats, or reporting structures for upward communication, the template becomes the goal. Contributors optimize for satisfying the format rather than conveying what matters. The process designed to surface information becomes the obstacle to surfacing information.
