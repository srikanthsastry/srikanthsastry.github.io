**AI shortcuts the engineering apprenticeship, shrinking the pool of humans capable of maintaining guardrails.** Charity Majors' observation that software engineering is a 7-year apprenticeship lands differently in the context of AI-assisted development. Guardrails are built and maintained by engineers who understand *why* they exist. If AI-assisted juniors never develop that understanding because the AI shortcut the learning process, the guardrail-maintenance pool shrinks with each generation.

The system eventually reaches a state where the agents that erode guardrails are the primary code producers, the humans who would maintain guardrails never learned how, and the formal verification layer that could substitute for human judgment was never built because it was too expensive at the time.
