**AI-generated code entering an open source repository makes the codebase unauditable, forcing corporations to either ban AI contributions or close the source.** Some files are copyrighted and licensed, others legally unowned, others legally contestable "AI-assisted" code. Under quarterly pressure, no team chooses "slower but cleanly copyrighted." The forced choice is binary: accept the velocity penalty or abandon openness. MongoDB, Elastic, HashiCorp, and Redis already demonstrated the pattern with cloud vendors. AI is a larger threat because cloud vendors merely underpriced the business model. AI dissolves the legal mechanism that made the licenses mean anything.
