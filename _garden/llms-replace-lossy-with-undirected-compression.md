**LLMs replace lossy compression with undirected compression.**

The counterargument that generative AI can bypass the broken reporting chain fails on four counts. First, executives who don't understand the domain ask wrong questions and get confident wrong answers — the "what to ask" problem. Second, the LLM is a Suggestible Actor: intentionless, locally reasoning, susceptible to framing, confabulates under uncertainty. Third, tacit knowledge — the most valuable kind — was never written down, so there's nothing for the LLM to traverse. Fourth, LLM synthesis creates a false sense of completeness: it *reads* like a comprehensive answer, removing the executive's awareness they're deciding on partial information. That's more dangerous than an obviously filtered report.
